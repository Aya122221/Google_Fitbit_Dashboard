# Google_Fitbit_Dashboard
Power BI Marketing Analytics Dashboard

📊 Overview

This project is an interactive Power BI marketing analytics dashboard designed to analyze marketing and sales performance and turn raw data into meaningful business insights.

The dashboard provides an overview of key performance indicators and allows users to explore performance across different dimensions, including advertising platforms, product categories, customer age groups, countries, and time periods.

This project was developed as part of my Data Analytics training at the National Telecommunication Institute (NTI).

🎯 Objectives

The main objectives of this project were to:

Analyze overall marketing and sales performance.
Track key business and marketing KPIs.
Understand conversion and advertising performance.
Compare revenue generated by different advertising platforms.
Analyze revenue across product categories.
Explore customer behavior across different age groups.
Examine performance across countries and time periods.
Present the results through interactive Power BI dashboards.
📈 Dashboard Pages
1. Home Page

The Home Page serves as the main navigation page of the dashboard and provides access to the different sections of the report.

2. Overview

The Overview page provides a high-level summary of marketing performance through KPIs and visualizations.

Key metrics and analyses include:

Total Revenue
Total Impressions
Total Clicks
Total Conversions
Average Order Value
Revenue per Click
Revenue by Advertising Platform
Conversions by Age Group
Marketing conversion funnel
3. Analysis

The Analysis page provides a deeper look into revenue and customer-related performance.

The analysis includes:

Revenue by Product Category
Revenue by Age Group and Time
Revenue by Time Period
Average Order Value
Total Conversions
Revenue per Click
Additional KPI-based analysis
4. Relation

The Relation page focuses on relationships between marketing activities and performance across different dimensions.

The visualizations include:

Conversion Rate by Advertising Platform
Total Conversions by Advertising Platform
Conversion Value by Country
Revenue per Click
Average Order Value
Total Revenue
📊 Key KPIs

The dashboard uses several calculated measures to evaluate performance, including:

Total Revenue
Total Conversions
Total Clicks
Total Impressions
Average Order Value
Revenue per Click
Conversion Rate

These KPIs help provide a comprehensive view of the marketing funnel, from impressions and clicks to conversions and revenue.
